# Review-request messages (for the clinic to send, not automated)

Reviews that name the **service and the area** help most in local and AI search
(for example "rabbit spay in Pimple Nilakh"). Never write reviews for customers, offer
anything in exchange for one, or ask only happy customers (Google's policy forbids selective
review gating). Send one message, once, after the visit.

Google review link: open the clinic's Google Business Profile → **Ask for reviews** → copy the short link,
and paste it where it says `<GOOGLE REVIEW LINK>` below.

---

## 1. General visit (WhatsApp, English)

> Hi {name}, thank you for bringing {pet name} to Shivraj Pet Clinic & Lab today. We hope {he/she} feels better soon.
> If you have a minute, a Google review about your visit would really help other pet parents in Pune find us. It helps most if you mention what {pet name} came in for:
> <GOOGLE REVIEW LINK>
> – Dr. Pritesh Vidhate & team

## 2. Exotic pet visit (rabbit / bird / turtle / guinea pig)

> Hi {name}, thank you for trusting us with {pet name}. Finding a vet for {rabbits/birds/turtles} in Pune isn't easy, and other owners often ask us how they can find one.
> If you're happy to, please share your experience on Google, mentioning the kind of pet and what we treated:
> <GOOGLE REVIEW LINK>
> Shivraj Pet Clinic & Lab, Pimple Nilakh

## 3. After surgery (send at the suture check, not on surgery day)

> Hi {name}, good to see {pet name} healing well at today's check. If you'd like to tell other pet parents about the {procedure} and recovery, a Google review would mean a lot to the team:
> <GOOGLE REVIEW LINK>

## 4. Lab / diagnostics visit

> Hi {name}, thanks for visiting Shivraj Pet Clinic & Lab. If having {pet name}'s blood test results within the visit helped, other pet parents would find your review useful:

## Replying to reviews

Reply to every review within a few days, by name, without sharing medical details:

- **Positive:** "Thank you, {name}! We're glad {pet name}'s {visit/surgery} went well. See you at the next check-up. – Dr. Pritesh"
- **Negative:** "We're sorry to hear this, {name}. We'd like to understand what happened. Please call us on +91 77569 65169 so we can talk it through. – Dr. Pritesh"
