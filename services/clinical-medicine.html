What Happens at a Consultation

At Shivraj Pet Clinic & Lab, every consultation follows a structured approach that leaves nothing to chance. Dr. Pritesh reviews the history, performs a complete physical examination from nose to tail, and integrates findings from any diagnostics run the same day.

You receive a clear, honest explanation of what was found, what it means, what the treatment options are, and what to watch for at home. We don't rush appointments and we encourage questions — because an informed pet owner is the most important part of any treatment plan.

What Conditions Are Commonly Treated?

Dermatology (Skin & Coat)

  • Allergic skin disease — food allergies, environmental atopy, flea allergy dermatitis
  • Pyoderma (bacterial skin infection)
  • Mange — Demodex and Sarcoptes mite infestations
  • Ringworm (dermatophytosis)
  • Hot spots (acute moist dermatitis)
  • Seborrhoea and scaling conditions

Gastrointestinal

  • Vomiting and diarrhea — acute and chronic
  • Pancreatitis
  • Inflammatory bowel disease (IBD)
  • Constipation and megacolon in cats
  • Parvovirus and other intestinal infections
  • Liver disease — hepatitis, fatty liver, portosystemic shunts

Respiratory

  • Kennel cough (infectious tracheobronchitis)
  • Pneumonia — bacterial, viral, fungal
  • Feline upper respiratory complex (cat flu)
  • Tracheal collapse in small breeds
  • Pleural effusion (fluid around lungs)

Urinary

  • Urinary tract infections (UTI)
  • Feline lower urinary tract disease (FLUTD)
  • Urinary blockage in male cats — emergency
  • Chronic kidney disease (CKD) management
  • Bladder stones

Endocrine / Hormonal

  • Diabetes mellitus — insulin management in dogs and cats
  • Cushing's disease (hyperadrenocorticism)
  • Hypothyroidism in dogs
  • Hyperthyroidism in cats
  • Addison's disease

Neurological

  • Seizures — diagnosis and long-term management
  • Disc disease and spinal pain
  • Vestibular disease (sudden head tilt and loss of balance)
💊 Second opinions welcome: If your pet has received a diagnosis elsewhere that you're unsure about, Dr. Pritesh is happy to review records, repeat examination, and provide an independent assessment. We encourage it — good medicine benefits from scrutiny.

Frequently asked questions

What does a consultation cost at Shivraj Pet Clinic & Lab?

Consultation fee is ₹300, payable at the clinic. All costs for diagnostics or treatment are discussed and agreed upfront — no surprise bills.

Can I come without an appointment?

Walk-ins are accepted during clinic hours. For complex cases or if you want to ensure a specific time slot, calling ahead is recommended. Emergencies are always seen immediately.

Do you treat cats and exotic animals, not just dogs?

Yes — Dr. Pritesh treats dogs, cats, birds, rabbits, turtles, guinea pigs, hamsters, and other exotic species. We're one of the few clinics in PCMC with this range of expertise.

What if my pet needs multiple tests the same day?

We prefer to complete diagnostics in one visit where possible. Our in-house lab, X-ray, and ultrasound mean most diagnostic workups can be done same-day without referral.

Can I get a second opinion from Dr. Pritesh on a serious diagnosis?

Absolutely. We encourage second opinions for cancer, neurological disease, organ failure, and any condition where the diagnosis will significantly change the pet's management. Bring previous records and we'll review them honestly.
