When to bring a small pet in

Guinea pigs and hamsters are prey animals and hide pain well. Call the clinic the same day if your pet:

  • has stopped eating; guinea pigs should never go long without food
  • is drooling, dropping food or losing weight, which often points to overgrown teeth
  • has severe diarrhoea (in hamsters, wet tail can be fatal within about 48 hours)
  • has blood in the urine or strains to urinate, a sign of bladder stones
  • is breathing with difficulty, very weak, or injured

What we treat

  • Dental malocclusion: overgrown or misaligned teeth in guinea pigs and hamsters.
  • Vitamin C deficiency (scurvy): guinea pigs cannot make their own vitamin C; deficiency causes weakness, joint pain and poor healing.
  • Wet tail in hamsters, and cheek pouch impaction.
  • Bladder stones, which are relatively common in guinea pigs.
  • Bumblefoot: painful foot infections from pressure sores, obesity or unsuitable flooring.
  • Skin conditions, parasites and weight loss.

Small mammals can safely have surgery when it is medically needed, with gaseous anaesthesia and monitoring. Blood tests and X-rays are done at the same clinic.

Daily diet

Guinea pigs need good-quality hay, fresh vegetables rich in vitamin C, fresh water and guinea pig-specific pellets every day. An annual check-up is recommended, and more often for older pets or those with a long-term condition.

Frequently asked questions

Do you treat guinea pigs and hamsters in Pune?

Yes. Guinea pigs, hamsters and gerbils are seen at our Exotic Animal Centre in Pimple Nilakh, Pune. The exotic consultation is ₹400.

Why has my guinea pig stopped eating?

Usually dental disease, pain or a digestive problem, and it needs a vet visit the same day because guinea pigs should never go long without eating.

Why do guinea pigs need vitamin C?

Because guinea pigs cannot make vitamin C themselves. Without it in the diet they develop scurvy, with weakness, joint pain and poor wound healing.

What is wet tail in hamsters?

Wet tail is severe diarrhoea in hamsters. It can be fatal within about 48 hours without treatment, so bring your hamster in straight away.

Can guinea pigs have surgery safely?

Yes. With proper anaesthesia and monitoring, guinea pigs can safely have surgery when it is medically necessary.
