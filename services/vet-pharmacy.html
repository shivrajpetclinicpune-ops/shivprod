Why an In-House Pharmacy Matters

When a diagnosis is made, treatment should start immediately — not tomorrow, after a trip to a human pharmacy to hunt for a veterinary prescription. At Shivraj Pet Clinic & Lab, our in-house pharmacy stocks the complete range of medicines for dogs, cats, and exotic animals, allowing Dr. Pritesh to dispense prescription medicines directly at the consultation.

This is particularly important for acute conditions — a dog diagnosed with a urinary blockage, a cat with a severe respiratory infection, a rabbit with GI stasis — where same-day treatment is critical to outcomes.

What Medicines and Supplies Do We Stock?

Prescription Medicines

  • Antibiotics — a complete range for skin, urinary, respiratory, gastrointestinal, and systemic infections
  • Anti-inflammatory pain medication (NSAIDs) — post-surgery, arthritis, injury
  • Corticosteroids — allergy management, immune-mediated disease
  • Anti-parasitic medications — for internal and external parasites
  • Cardiac medications — for heart failure management
  • Seizure medications — phenobarbitone, potassium bromide
  • Insulin — for diabetic dog and cat management
  • Thyroid medications — for hypothyroid dogs and hyperthyroid cats
  • Chemotherapy support medications

Parasite Control Products

  • Tick prevention — topical spot-ons, oral chewables, and tick collars for dogs and cats, recommended based on your pet's age, lifestyle and health status
  • Flea treatment — spot-ons and tablets for dogs and cats
  • Dewormers — puppy/kitten formulations and adult broadspectrum
  • Ear mite treatment
  • Mange treatment

Nutritional Supplements

  • Joint supplements (Glucosamine, Chondroitin, Omega-3) for arthritis
  • Liver support supplements (SAMe, Silymarin)
  • Probiotic and digestive enzyme supplements
  • Vitamin and mineral supplements for exotic animals
  • Kidney support diets and supplements

Specialty Items

  • Prescription therapeutic diets (kidney, liver, skin, gastrointestinal formulations)
  • Wound care supplies — antiseptics, bandaging materials
  • Elizabethan collars (e-collars) for post-operative care
  • Medicated shampoos for skin conditions
💊 Dispensing only under prescription: All prescription medications are dispensed only after examination and diagnosis by Dr. Pritesh. We don't dispense prescription medicines without a clinical reason — this protects your pet and prevents antimicrobial resistance.

Frequently asked questions

Can I buy tick prevention without a consultation?

Standard over-the-counter parasite control products (spot-ons, some tablets) can be purchased without a consultation. Prescription-only parasite control products (like some chewables) require a consultation first.

Do you sell therapeutic diets for kidney or liver disease?

Yes — we stock veterinary therapeutic diets for chronic kidney disease, liver disease, skin conditions, gastrointestinal disease, and obesity management.

Can you provide medicines for exotic pets like rabbits and birds?

Yes. We carry medications specifically dosed and formulated for exotic species, which often require different formulations than those used for dogs and cats.

My dog is on long-term medication — can I just pick it up without a new consultation each time?

For stable long-term conditions, we can arrange repeat dispensing after an initial assessment. However, annual or biannual check-ins are recommended to ensure the medication is still appropriate and the dose is correct.
