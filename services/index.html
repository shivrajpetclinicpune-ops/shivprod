Care & services

Veterinary services in Pimple Nilakh, Pune.

Everyday care, a dedicated Exotic Animal Centre, same-building diagnostics and surgery under gaseous anaesthesia, for dogs, cats, birds, rabbits, turtles and more.

Prevent · Treat · Supply

Everyday care

  • Preventive MedicineVaccination schedules, deworming and wellness checks for dogs, cats and exotic pets.
  • Clinical MedicineConsultations, diagnosis and treatment plans. Walk-ins accepted, second opinions welcome.
  • Vet PharmacyPrescription medicines, parasite control, supplements, therapeutic diets and e-collars on site.

Birds · Rabbits · Reptiles

Exotic Animal Centre

  • Exotic Animal CentreBirds, rabbits, turtles, reptiles, guinea pigs and hamsters, examined, diagnosed and operated on with species-appropriate care and gaseous anaesthesia.
Species
Birds · Rabbits · Turtles & tortoises · Iguanas & lizards · Guinea pigs, hamsters & gerbils

Lab · X-ray · Ultrasound

Diagnostics

  • Pathology LabIn-house blood panels, urinalysis and cytology. CBC and biochemistry ready in 15–20 minutes.
  • Digital X-RayOn-site digital radiography with instant images, for dogs, cats and exotic patients alike.
  • SonographyUltrasound imaging at the clinic, including pregnancy confirmation.

Gaseous anaesthesia

Surgery

  • Dog NeuteringRoutine same-day neutering under gas anaesthesia, with pre-surgery bloodwork in our own lab.
  • Cat SpayingSafe spaying for female cats, with a 10-day suture check.
  • Soft Tissue SurgeryHernia, cherry eye, anal gland and bladder surgery.
  • Orthopedic SurgeryFracture repair with plating, intramedullary pinning and external fixation.
  • Dog Tumor SurgeryLump evaluation by fine-needle aspirate, surgical removal and histopathology.
  • Foreign Body RemovalX-ray confirmation and removal of swallowed objects.
  • Emergency SurgeryGDV, trauma and C-sections during opening hours. Call the clinic first so the team can prepare.

Need CT or MRI? We coordinate referrals for advanced imaging.
