Doctors & team

Two generations. One clinic.

Shivraj Pet Clinic & Lab is a father-and-son veterinary practice in Pimple Nilakh, Pune, caring for dogs, cats, birds, rabbits, turtles and exotic animals since 2013.

Our story

  1. Before 2013

    Dr. Shivaji B. Vidhate serves in field and companion animal medicine, retiring as District Animal Husbandry Officer, Pune.

  2. 2013

    Shivraj Pet Clinic & Lab opens in Pimple Nilakh.

  3. Today

    A two-generation practice with an in-house pathology lab, digital X-ray, sonography, surgery under gaseous anaesthesia and a dedicated Exotic Animal Centre.

Founder & Senior Veterinarian

Dr. Shivaji B. Vidhate

M.V.Sc. (Pathology) · 33+ years of experience
MSVC Reg. No. 3182

Retired District Animal Husbandry Officer, Pune, with 33+ years of experience in field and companion animal medicine and surgery. He founded Shivraj Pet Clinic & Lab in 2013 and remains the clinic’s pathology anchor.

Focus Pathology, Field medicine, Surgery, Retired DAHO, Pune

Head Veterinarian
