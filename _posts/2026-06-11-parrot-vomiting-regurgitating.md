<p>Parrots regurgitate as a normal bonding behavior toward favored people or toys — which makes distinguishing it from illness-related vomiting genuinely tricky for new owners.</p>

<h2>Normal Regurgitation</h2>
<p>Head-bobbing before regurgitating onto a favorite person, toy, or mirror is typically an affectionate, hormonal, or bonding behavior.</p>

<h2>Concerning Vomiting</h2>
<p>Vomiting without the typical bonding context, combined with lethargy, fluffing, or reduced appetite, points toward illness rather than normal behavior.</p>

<h2>When to See a Vet</h2>
<p>If vomiting is frequent, unprompted by a bonding context, or paired with other symptoms, schedule an evaluation.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>Is regurgitating onto toys normal?</strong><br>Yes, this is a common bonding-related behavior in many parrot species.</p>
<p><strong>How can I tell illness-related vomiting apart?</strong><br>Look at context and accompanying symptoms — illness-related vomiting isn't tied to bonding behavior and often comes with lethargy.</p>
<p><strong>Can this behavior be discouraged if excessive?</strong><br>Ask your vet or an avian behaviorist for guidance if it becomes disruptive.</p>
