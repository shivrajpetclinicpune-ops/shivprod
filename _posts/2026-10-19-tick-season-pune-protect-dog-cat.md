<p>Tick prevention works best as a proactive, ongoing strategy rather than a reaction after you've already spotted one on your pet.</p>

<h2>Local Tick Activity</h2>
<p>Ticks can remain active across much of the year in Pune's climate, making year-round rather than purely seasonal prevention the safer approach.</p>

<h2>Practical Prevention Steps</h2>
<p>Consistent use of a vet-recommended tick prevention product, regular coat checks after outdoor time, and maintaining a tidy yard or outdoor space all help.</p>

<h2>Why This Matters Beyond Irritation</h2>
<p>Ticks can transmit tick fever and other serious diseases, as covered in our tick fever article — prevention protects against more than just the bite itself.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>**How often should I check my pet for ticks?**</strong><br>After any significant outdoor time, particularly in grassy or wooded areas.</p>
<p><strong>**Is tick prevention needed year-round in Pune?**</strong><br>Given the local climate, yes, continuous prevention is generally the safer approach.</p>
<p><strong>**What should I do if I find a tick?**</strong><br>Ask your vet for the correct, safe removal method rather than attempting removal with an incorrect technique.</p>
