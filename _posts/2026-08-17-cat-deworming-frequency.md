<p>Cat deworming frequency, similar to dogs, depends heavily on lifestyle factors rather than a single universal rule.</p>

<h2>General Guidance</h2>
<p>Indoor cats may need deworming less frequently than outdoor cats, but most vets still recommend periodic deworming, often every 3-6 months, depending on risk.</p>

<h2>Outdoor and Hunting Cats</h2>
<p>Cats that hunt or spend significant time outdoors are at higher parasite exposure risk and may need more frequent deworming.</p>

<h2>Working with Your Vet</h2>
<p>A personalized schedule based on your cat's specific lifestyle is more effective than a generic universal interval.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>Do indoor cats really need deworming?</strong><br>Generally yes, though frequency may be lower than for outdoor cats — ask your vet.</p>
<p><strong>Can fleas be linked to certain parasites?</strong><br>Yes, tapeworms specifically can be transmitted via fleas, linking flea control to deworming needs.</p>
<p><strong>Is deworming necessary even with no visible symptoms?</strong><br>Yes, parasites aren't always visibly obvious.</p>
