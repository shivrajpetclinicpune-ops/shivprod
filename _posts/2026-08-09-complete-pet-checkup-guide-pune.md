<p>This guide pulls together what pet checkups should involve across a dog or cat's life, tailored to what's relevant for Pune-based owners specifically.</p>

<h2>Puppy and Kitten Stage</h2>
<p>Frequent visits for vaccination, deworming, and growth monitoring characterize this early stage of care.</p>

<h2>Adult Stage</h2>
<p>Annual wellness checkups, ongoing preventive care, and monitoring for any emerging concerns define this stage.</p>

<h2>Senior Stage</h2>
<p>More frequent checkups, often every six months, with added bloodwork and closer monitoring for age-related conditions.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>**Does checkup frequency really change that much by life stage?**</strong><br>Yes, from frequent puppy/kitten visits to occasional adult checkups to more frequent senior monitoring, the pattern shifts meaningfully.</p>
<p><strong>**Is this guide different for Pune specifically?**</strong><br>Local climate and disease prevalence factor into specific preventive recommendations — ask your vet for Pune-specific guidance.</p>
