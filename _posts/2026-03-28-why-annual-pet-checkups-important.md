<p>"They look completely fine" is exactly the gap annual checkups are designed to close — many conditions develop silently long before visible symptoms appear.</p>

<h2>What a Checkup Catches Early</h2>
<p>Weight trends, dental disease, early organ changes, and subtle physical exam findings can all be caught before they become obvious problems.</p>

<h2>Why Waiting for Symptoms Is Too Late for Some Conditions</h2>
<p>Several chronic conditions are more manageable, and less costly to treat, when caught early rather than after symptoms force a reactive visit.</p>

<h2>What a Basic Annual Visit Covers</h2>
<p>A physical exam, weight and body condition check, and a conversation about vaccination, parasite prevention, and any concerns are standard components.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>Is an annual visit enough for a young healthy pet?</strong><br>Generally yes, though senior pets often benefit from more frequent visits.</p>
<p><strong>What if I can't afford a full workup every year?</strong><br>Discuss options with your vet — even a basic exam-only visit provides meaningful early-detection value.</p>
<p><strong>Does pet insurance typically cover annual checkups?</strong><br>This varies by policy — check your specific coverage.</p>
