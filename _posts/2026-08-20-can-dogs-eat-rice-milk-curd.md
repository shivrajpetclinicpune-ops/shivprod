<p>These common Indian household staples come up often in dog diet questions — here's the practical answer for each.</p>

<h2>Rice</h2>
<p>Plain, cooked rice is generally safe and often used as a bland diet component for dogs with mild digestive upset.</p>

<h2>Milk</h2>
<p>Many adult dogs are lactose intolerant to varying degrees — milk can cause digestive upset and isn't necessary in a balanced diet.</p>

<h2>Curd (Yogurt)</h2>
<p>Plain curd in moderation is often better tolerated than milk due to lower lactose content, but introduce gradually and watch for reaction.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>**Is rice good for dogs with an upset stomach?**</strong><br>Plain, cooked rice is commonly used as part of a bland diet during mild digestive upset — check with your vet for the full approach.</p>
<p><strong>**Why is milk different from curd for dogs?**</strong><br>Curd generally has lower lactose content, making it easier for some dogs to tolerate than plain milk.</p>
<p><strong>**Should I introduce these foods gradually?**</strong><br>Yes, introduce any new food gradually and watch for digestive reaction.</p>
