<p>Feather loss covers a wide range from completely normal to seriously concerning — the pattern and context are what tell the real story.</p>

<h2>Normal Molting</h2>
<p>Gradual, symmetrical feather loss and regrowth is a normal, periodic process for most parrot species.</p>

<h2>Concerning Patterns</h2>
<p>Bald patches, feathers that don't regrow, plucking behavior, or feather loss combined with skin irritation point toward a medical or behavioral issue needing evaluation.</p>

<h2>Why a Workup Matters</h2>
<p>As covered in our Parrot Diseases guide, feather problems can stem from nutrition, stress, parasites, or underlying illness — identifying which one matters for treatment.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>Is molting seasonal?</strong><br>It can follow seasonal patterns in some species, but should still be gradual and symmetrical.</p>
<p><strong>Can nutritional deficiency cause feather loss?</strong><br>Yes, particularly with an unbalanced, seed-heavy diet.</p>
<p><strong>When should feather loss prompt a vet visit?</strong><br>When it's not symmetrical, doesn't regrow, or is combined with skin irritation or behavior changes.</p>
