<p>Occasional scratching is normal; constant, persistent scratching is your dog telling you something specific is bothering them.</p>

<h2>Common Causes</h2>
<p>Allergies (food or environmental), fleas, dry skin, and infections are the most common causes of persistent scratching.</p>

<h2>What to Check</h2>
<p>Look for visible parasites, redness, hair loss, or skin changes in the areas being scratched — this can help your vet narrow down the cause faster.</p>

<h2>When to See a Vet</h2>
<p>Persistent scratching lasting more than a few days, or accompanied by visible skin changes, warrants a veterinary evaluation rather than continued home management.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>Can seasonal changes affect scratching?</strong><br>Yes, environmental allergies often have seasonal patterns.</p>
<p><strong>Is scratching always allergy-related?</strong><br>No, parasites and dry skin are also common causes worth ruling out.</p>
<p><strong>Can diet affect skin itchiness?</strong><br>Yes, food allergies or sensitivities are a recognized cause of persistent scratching.</p>
