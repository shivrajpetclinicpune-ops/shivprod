<p>Overfeeding and underfeeding are both real risks during the rapid growth period — getting portions right matters more than in adulthood.</p>

<h2>Why This Differs from Adult Feeding</h2>
<p>Growing puppies and kittens have higher caloric and nutritional needs relative to body size than adult pets, requiring more frequent, carefully portioned meals.</p>

<h2>Using Food Packaging Guidelines Correctly</h2>
<p>Package guidelines are a starting point based on current weight and expected growth, not a fixed rule — adjust based on actual body condition.</p>

<h2>Working with Your Vet</h2>
<p>Regular weigh-ins during growth help fine-tune portions to your specific pet's actual growth trajectory, not just generic guidelines.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>**Should I free-feed a growing puppy or kitten?**</strong><br>Scheduled meals are generally recommended over free-feeding to monitor intake and prevent overfeeding.</p>
<p><strong>**How often should growing pets be weighed?**</strong><br>Regularly during rapid growth phases — ask your vet for a specific schedule.</p>
<p><strong>**Can overfeeding during growth cause long-term issues?**</strong><br>Yes, particularly in large-breed puppies, where growth rate management matters for joint development.</p>
