<p>A veterinary pathology lab is where much of the actual diagnostic detective work happens — beyond what a physical exam alone can reveal.</p>

<h2>What a Pathology Lab Does</h2>
<p>Processes and analyzes blood, tissue, and fluid samples to identify infections, cellular abnormalities, and organ function issues.</p>

<h2>Why Access Matters</h2>
<p>A clinic with strong in-house or closely connected pathology support can turn around results faster, supporting quicker treatment decisions.</p>

<h2>Beyond Blood Tests</h2>
<p>Pathology labs also handle biopsy analysis, cytology, and other specialized diagnostic work beyond routine bloodwork.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>Is pathology only relevant for cancer diagnosis?</strong><br>No, it covers a wide range of diagnostic needs beyond cancer, including infections and organ disease.</p>
<p><strong>Do all clinics have in-house pathology labs?</strong><br>No, capabilities vary significantly by clinic — ask about specific in-house testing available.</p>
<p><strong>How does this affect cost?</strong><br>In-house capability can sometimes reduce turnaround time without necessarily increasing cost — ask your clinic for specifics.</p>
