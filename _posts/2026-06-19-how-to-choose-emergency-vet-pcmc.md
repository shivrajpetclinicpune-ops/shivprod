<p>A great clinic for routine care isn't automatically your best emergency option — emergency readiness is a distinct set of capabilities worth checking in advance.</p>

<h2>What to Verify in Advance</h2>
<p>Emergency hours or on-call availability, in-house diagnostic capability, and the clinic's ability to handle unstable or critical patients.</p>

<h2>Why Advance Research Matters</h2>
<p>Researching this before an emergency happens means you're not scrambling to find options while your pet is in crisis.</p>

<h2>Save the Information Now</h2>
<p>Keep your chosen emergency clinic's contact information easily accessible — in your phone, not just somewhere you'd have to search for it.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>Should my regular vet also be my emergency option?</strong><br>Only if they offer genuine emergency capability — confirm this rather than assuming.</p>
<p><strong>What if my regular clinic is closed during an emergency?</strong><br>Have a backup emergency option identified and saved in advance.</p>
<p><strong>Is calling ahead always possible in an emergency?</strong><br>When possible, yes — it helps the clinic prepare, though getting there safely takes priority.</p>
