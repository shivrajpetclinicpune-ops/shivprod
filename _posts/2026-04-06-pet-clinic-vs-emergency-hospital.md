<p>These terms get used loosely, but the actual distinction matters when you're deciding where to take a pet in distress.</p>

<h2>Regular Pet Clinic</h2>
<p>Typically handles routine wellness, vaccination, diagnostics, and many urgent (but not always after-hours emergency) cases.</p>

<h2>Emergency Veterinary Hospital</h2>
<p>Generally equipped for after-hours, critical, or highly complex cases, often with more extensive on-site facilities for intensive care.</p>

<h2>Knowing Which to Use When</h2>
<p>For routine and many urgent daytime concerns, your regular clinic is often appropriate; for true after-hours emergencies, confirm in advance which option applies.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>Can my regular clinic handle emergencies during business hours?</strong><br>Many can — confirm their specific emergency capability directly.</p>
<p><strong>Is an emergency hospital always more expensive?</strong><br>Often somewhat, given more extensive facilities, but this varies — the priority in a real emergency should be getting appropriate care.</p>
<p><strong>Should I have both options identified in advance?</strong><br>Yes, this avoids scrambling to decide during an actual crisis.</p>
