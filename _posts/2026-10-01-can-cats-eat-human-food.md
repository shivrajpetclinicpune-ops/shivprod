Cats are **obligate carnivores**. Their bodies need nutrients found naturally only in animal tissue, such as **taurine**, preformed vitamin A and arachidonic acid. A cat fed mainly on rice, chapati and milk, which still happens in many Indian homes, can develop heart disease (dilated cardiomyopathy), blindness and poor growth.

So the real question is not whether cats can eat human food. It is which small extras are safe alongside a complete cat diet.

## Safe in small amounts

- **Plain boiled chicken** (no skin, no bones, no masala).
- **Plain boiled or steamed fish** (bones removed), occasionally. Don't feed fish every day, and never raw fish regularly. Some raw fish destroys vitamin B1 (thiamine).
- **Plain cooked egg**, scrambled or boiled with no oil or salt.
- **A little plain paneer or curd** for cats that tolerate dairy. Many adult cats are lactose intolerant.
- **Cooked pumpkin**, a teaspoon, which some cats like and which helps with fibre.

Treats and extras should be **no more than about 10%** of daily calories. The rest should be a complete and balanced cat food, wet, dry or both.

## Dangerous or toxic for cats

| Food or item | Why |
|---|---|
| **Onion, garlic, spring onion, chives** (including in gravy, dal tadka, biryani masala) | Destroy red blood cells. Cats are more sensitive than dogs. |
| **Grapes and raisins (kishmish)** | Linked to kidney injury in dogs, and avoided in cats too |
| **Chocolate, coffee, tea** | Theobromine and caffeine: heart and nervous system effects |
| **Alcohol, raw dough** | Very toxic even in small amounts |
| **Cooked bones** (chicken or fish) | Splinter, causing choking or gut injury |
| **Large amounts of milk** | Diarrhoea in lactose-intolerant cats |
| **Salty snacks** (namkeen, chips) | Salt overload |
| **Xylitol** (sugar-free gum, some peanut butter) | Better avoided entirely |
| **Dog food as the main diet** | Lacks the taurine and protein levels cats need |

## Not food, but often eaten

- **Lilies** (Asiatic lilies, tiger lilies, Easter lilies), including the pollen and the vase water, cause **kidney failure in cats**. Even licking pollen off the fur is dangerous. Don't keep lilies in a home with a cat.
- **Paracetamol (Crocin, Dolo) is deadly to cats.** One tablet can kill. Never give any human medicine.
- Essential oils, naphthalene balls, rat poison and floor cleaners containing phenol.

## Common myths

**"Cats need milk."** Kittens need their mother's milk or kitten formula. Adult cats need fresh water, not milk.

**"A vegetarian diet is fine if we're vegetarian."** Cats cannot be safely vegetarian. Their diet needs to be meat-based.

**"Fish is the best food for cats."** Fish is fine sometimes, but an all-fish diet can cause deficiencies and makes some cats picky.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**My cat steals curd rice. Is that harmful?**\
A little plain curd rice won't harm most cats, but it should not replace cat food. Diarrhoea afterwards means your cat doesn't tolerate dairy.

**Can I make home-cooked food for my cat?**\
It is possible but difficult to balance correctly. It needs a vet-designed recipe with added taurine, calcium and vitamins. Don't attempt it from internet recipes.

**How do I stop my cat begging at the table?**\
Feed measured meals at fixed times, give treats only from the cat's own food, and don't feed from your plate.
