<p>Rabies prevention in India carries particular importance given the disease's continued presence — responsible pet ownership plays a real role in broader public health.</p>

<h2>Vaccination as the Core Strategy</h2>
<p>Keeping your pet's rabies vaccination current is the single most effective prevention step available to pet owners.</p>

<h2>Avoiding Exposure</h2>
<p>Limiting your pet's contact with unknown or stray animals reduces exposure risk on top of vaccination protection.</p>

<h2>What to Do After a Bite or Suspected Exposure</h2>
<p>Seek veterinary care immediately for your pet, and seek medical attention immediately for any human bitten by a potentially rabid animal — regardless of vaccination status.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>**How often does the rabies vaccine need renewal?**</strong><br>This depends on the specific vaccine and local regulations — ask your vet for the current schedule.</p>
<p><strong>**Should stray animal contact always be avoided?**</strong><br>Yes, minimizing contact with unknown animals is a sensible precaution regardless of your own pet's vaccination status.</p>
<p><strong>**Is rabies still a real risk in Pune specifically?**</strong><br>Regional risk varies — your vet can advise on current local considerations.</p>
