<p>Most common rabbit diseases trace back to a handful of preventable root causes — diet, dental care, and environment — making prevention genuinely achievable with the right habits.</p>

<h2>The Most Common Conditions</h2>
<p>GI stasis, dental disease, respiratory infections, and parasites account for the majority of rabbit health problems seen in practice.</p>

<h2>Prevention Through Diet</h2>
<p>A hay-based diet addresses both dental wear and gut motility simultaneously — arguably the single most impactful prevention step available.</p>

<h2>Prevention Through Routine Care</h2>
<p>Regular checkups, clean housing, and monitoring appetite and droppings daily catch problems before they escalate.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>Is diet really the biggest prevention factor?</strong><br>For the most common conditions, yes — hay-based nutrition addresses multiple risk factors at once.</p>
<p><strong>How often should rabbits see a vet for prevention?</strong><br>At least annually, more often for seniors.</p>
<p><strong>Can indoor rabbits still get parasites?</strong><br>Yes, indoor status reduces but doesn't eliminate risk.</p>
