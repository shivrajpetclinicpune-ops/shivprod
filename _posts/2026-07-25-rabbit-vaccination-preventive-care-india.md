<p>Rabbit vaccination protocols vary significantly by region — what's standard advice in the UK or US doesn't always translate directly to India, so local veterinary guidance matters here.</p>

<h2>Regional Differences</h2>
<p>Certain rabbit diseases common abroad (like myxomatosis) are less relevant in India, which changes what preventive vaccination is actually recommended locally.</p>

<h2>What Preventive Care Includes Locally</h2>
<p>Regular checkups, parasite prevention, dental monitoring, and nutrition guidance form the core of rabbit preventive care in the Indian context.</p>

<h2>Why Local Vet Guidance Matters</h2>
<p>Ask your vet specifically what's relevant for rabbits in your region rather than following generic international advice.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>Do pet rabbits in India need routine vaccines?</strong><br>This depends on regional disease prevalence — ask your vet what's currently recommended.</p>
<p><strong>How often should rabbits have checkups?</strong><br>Generally at least annually, more often for seniors or those with ongoing issues.</p>
<p><strong>Is parasite prevention needed for indoor rabbits?</strong><br>Yes, indoor rabbits can still be exposed to parasites through various routes.</p>
