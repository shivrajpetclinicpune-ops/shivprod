<p>The gap between running a test and getting results directly affects how quickly a sick pet can start appropriate treatment — in-house diagnostics close that gap significantly.</p>

<h2>The Traditional Delay</h2>
<p>Sending samples to an external lab can introduce a wait of hours to days, delaying critical treatment decisions for unstable patients.</p>

<h2>What In-House Testing Changes</h2>
<p>Same-visit or same-day results allow the veterinary team to make informed decisions faster, particularly valuable in emergencies.</p>

<h2>Where It Matters Most</h2>
<p>Unstable, emergency, or rapidly deteriorating patients benefit the most from the speed of in-house diagnostics.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>Does in-house testing cover everything an external lab does?</strong><br>Not always — some specialized tests still require external labs, but common core panels are frequently available in-house.</p>
<p><strong>Is in-house testing as accurate as external lab testing?</strong><br>Quality in-house equipment provides reliable results for the panels it covers.</p>
<p><strong>Does speed compromise accuracy?</strong><br>No, speed and accuracy aren't inherently in conflict with proper equipment and protocols.</p>
