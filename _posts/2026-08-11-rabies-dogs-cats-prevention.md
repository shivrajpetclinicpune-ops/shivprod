<p>Rabies is one of the most serious, and most preventable, diseases affecting dogs and cats — vaccination remains the single most effective protection available.</p>

<h2>Why Vaccination Is Essential</h2>
<p>Rabies is fatal once symptoms appear, in both animals and humans, which makes prevention through vaccination the only truly effective strategy.</p>

<h2>Recognizing Possible Symptoms</h2>
<p>Behavioral changes, aggression or unusual timidity, excessive drooling, and difficulty swallowing can be signs — any suspected case needs immediate professional attention, not home assessment.</p>

<h2>Prevention Beyond Vaccination</h2>
<p>Avoiding contact between your pet and unknown or stray animals, and keeping vaccination current, are important additional layers of protection.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>How often does the rabies vaccine need boosting?</strong><br>This depends on the specific vaccine and local regulations — ask your vet for the current schedule.</p>
<p><strong>What should I do if my pet is bitten by an unknown animal?</strong><br>Seek veterinary attention immediately, regardless of your pet's vaccination status.</p>
<p><strong>Is rabies vaccination legally required?</strong><br>It's strongly recommended and often required under local regulations — check current requirements with your vet.</p>
