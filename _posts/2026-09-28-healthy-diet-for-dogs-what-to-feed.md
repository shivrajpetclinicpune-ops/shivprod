<p>Dog nutrition marketing can be overwhelming — here's a grounded overview of what a genuinely balanced diet actually requires.</p>

<h2>Core Nutritional Needs</h2>
<p>A balanced diet needs appropriate protein, fat, carbohydrates, vitamins, and minerals in proportions suited to your dog's life stage and size.</p>

<h2>Commercial vs Home-Cooked</h2>
<p>Both can be done well or poorly — as covered in our home-cooked food article, balance and proper formulation matter more than the format itself.</p>

<h2>Individualizing the Plan</h2>
<p>Age, breed size, activity level, and any health conditions should all factor into your dog's specific dietary needs.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>**Is grain-free always better?**</strong><br>Not necessarily — this depends on your individual dog's needs, not a blanket rule.</p>
<p><strong>**How do I know if my dog's current diet is balanced?**</strong><br>Ask your vet to review it, particularly if home-cooking or considering a significant diet change.</p>
<p><strong>**Should diet change with age?**</strong><br>Yes, nutritional needs shift across puppy, adult, and senior life stages.</p>
