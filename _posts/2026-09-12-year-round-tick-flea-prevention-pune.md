<p>Pune's relatively warm climate means flea and tick risk doesn't meaningfully disappear in any season, unlike colder climates where winter offers a natural break.</p>

<h2>Why Local Climate Matters</h2>
<p>Warmer, humid conditions support flea and tick activity across most of the year in this region, reducing the value of seasonal-only prevention.</p>

<h2>Consistent Prevention</h2>
<p>Year-round use of a vet-recommended prevention product is more effective than starting and stopping based on assumed seasonal risk.</p>

<h2>Combining with Environmental Control</h2>
<p>Regular cleaning and monitoring of your pet's environment complements product-based prevention for the most effective overall protection.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>**Is prevention still needed during Pune's cooler months?**</strong><br>Yes, risk remains significant enough to warrant continuous prevention locally.</p>
<p><strong>**Can indoor-only pets skip prevention?**</strong><br>Generally not recommended to skip entirely, given how easily pests can be carried indoors.</p>
<p><strong>**Which prevention product is best?**</strong><br>This depends on your pet's specific needs — ask your vet for a personalized recommendation.</p>
