<p>Ear mites are tiny but cause outsized discomfort — and they're highly contagious between pets in the same household, making prompt treatment important.</p>

<h2>Symptoms</h2>
<p>Intense scratching at the ears, head shaking, dark, coffee-ground-like debris in the ear, and visible irritation are common signs.</p>

<h2>Why They Spread Easily</h2>
<p>Ear mites are highly contagious between pets in close contact, so multi-pet households often need to treat all animals, not just the symptomatic one.</p>

<h2>Treatment</h2>
<p>Vet-prescribed treatment is more effective and appropriate than over-the-counter options, and includes thorough ear cleaning as part of the process.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>Can ear mites spread to humans?</strong><br>This is uncommon, though not entirely impossible — good hygiene during treatment is still sensible.</p>
<p><strong>Should all pets in the household be treated?</strong><br>Often recommended given how contagious ear mites are between animals.</p>
<p><strong>How is a diagnosis confirmed?</strong><br>Your vet can typically confirm mites through a simple ear swab examination.</p>
