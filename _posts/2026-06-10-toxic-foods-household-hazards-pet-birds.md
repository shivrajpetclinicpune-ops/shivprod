<p>Birds are sensitive to substances that seem harmless around dogs and cats — a bird-safe home requires its own specific hazard checklist.</p>

<h2>Toxic Foods</h2>
<p>Avocado, chocolate, caffeine, alcohol, and high-salt or high-fat foods are all dangerous for birds.</p>

<h2>Household Hazards</h2>
<p>Non-stick cookware fumes (particularly when overheated), aerosols, scented candles, and certain cleaning products can be lethal to birds' sensitive respiratory systems.</p>

<h2>Environmental Awareness</h2>
<p>Because birds are so sensitive to airborne toxins, kitchen and cleaning habits often need adjustment in bird-owning households.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>Is non-stick cookware really dangerous for birds?</strong><br>Yes, overheated non-stick coatings can release fumes that are rapidly fatal to birds.</p>
<p><strong>Can scented candles affect birds?</strong><br>Yes, strong fragrances and fumes can affect their sensitive respiratory system.</p>
<p><strong>Are all human foods risky for birds?</strong><br>Not all, but several common ones are — check before sharing any new food.</p>
