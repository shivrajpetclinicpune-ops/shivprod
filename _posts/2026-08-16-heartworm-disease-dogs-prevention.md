<p>Heartworm disease, transmitted through mosquito bites, is a genuinely serious condition where prevention is significantly easier than treatment.</p>

<h2>How It Spreads</h2>
<p>Heartworm larvae are transmitted through mosquito bites, meaning any dog with mosquito exposure carries some risk.</p>

<h2>Why Prevention Is Preferred</h2>
<p>Prevention through regular medication is considerably simpler, safer, and less costly than treating an established heartworm infection.</p>

<h2>Detection</h2>
<p>Regular testing, as recommended by your vet, can catch infection before it becomes advanced, particularly important given the delayed symptom onset typical of this disease.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>**Is heartworm a concern in India?**</strong><br>Regional risk varies — ask your vet about local prevalence and appropriate prevention.</p>
<p><strong>**Can heartworm affect indoor dogs?**</strong><br>Yes, if mosquitoes have any access to the indoor environment.</p>
<p><strong>**How often should dogs be tested?**</strong><br>Your vet can advise a testing schedule appropriate to local risk and your dog's prevention status.</p>
