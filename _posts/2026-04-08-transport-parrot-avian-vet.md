<p>Birds are sensitive to temperature changes and stress in ways that make transport preparation genuinely important, not just a logistics afterthought.</p>

<h2>Use an Appropriate Carrier</h2>
<p>A secure, appropriately sized carrier — not a loose towel or open box — keeps your bird safe and contained during transport.</p>

<h2>Temperature Matters</h2>
<p>Keep the carrier warm, especially in cooler weather, since birds can lose body heat quickly, particularly when unwell.</p>

<h2>Minimize Stress</h2>
<p>A quiet, covered carrier and a calm car ride reduce anxiety, which matters more for birds than many other species.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>Should I cover the carrier completely during transport?</strong><br>Partial covering that still allows airflow is generally a good balance between calm and ventilation.</p>
<p><strong>Is it safe to bring food and water during a short trip?</strong><br>Usually unnecessary for short trips, but check with your vet for a sick or particularly fragile bird.</p>
<p><strong>What if my bird seems extremely stressed during transport?</strong><br>Keep the environment as calm and quiet as possible and get to the clinic promptly — your vet can advise afterward on reducing stress for future visits.</p>
