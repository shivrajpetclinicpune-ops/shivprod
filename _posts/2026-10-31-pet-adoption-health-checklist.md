<p>Adoption brings a lot to think about — this checklist focuses specifically on the health-related steps worth prioritizing early.</p>

<h2>Vaccination</h2>
<p>Establish or confirm vaccination status with your vet, restarting the schedule if history is unclear.</p>

<h2>Deworming</h2>
<p>Begin or continue an appropriate deworming schedule, particularly important given typically higher parasite exposure risk in rescue backgrounds.</p>

<h2>Baseline Testing</h2>
<p>Bloodwork and, depending on background, infectious disease screening help establish a clear health starting point for ongoing care.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>**Should this checklist be completed all at once?**</strong><br>Your vet will typically prioritize and sequence these appropriately across the first few visits rather than all in one appointment.</p>
<p><strong>**Does age affect what's prioritized first?**</strong><br>Yes, young pets often need vaccination and deworming addressed with particular urgency.</p>
<p><strong>**Is spaying or neutering part of this checklist?**</strong><br>Often discussed as part of overall health planning, timed appropriately based on age and individual factors.</p>
