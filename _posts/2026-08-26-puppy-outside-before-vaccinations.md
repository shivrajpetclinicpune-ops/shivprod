<p>This is a genuinely common tension — puppies need socialization early, but full vaccination protection takes weeks to build, creating a real balancing act.</p>

<h2>The Risk</h2>
<p>Until the vaccination series is far enough along, puppies are more vulnerable to certain serious diseases present in contaminated environments.</p>

<h2>A Balanced Approach</h2>
<p>Many vets recommend controlled, low-risk socialization — private, healthy-known dogs and clean environments — rather than full public dog parks before vaccination is more complete.</p>

<h2>Why Socialization Still Matters</h2>
<p>The early weeks are a critical socialization window, so avoiding all exposure isn't ideal either — the goal is managed exposure, not zero exposure.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>Is it safe to walk a puppy on a leash in public before full vaccination?</strong><br>Generally lower-risk than dog parks, but ask your vet for specific guidance based on your puppy's schedule.</p>
<p><strong>Can puppy classes happen before vaccination is complete?</strong><br>Some structured classes take precautions for this — ask about their health requirements for participants.</p>
<p><strong>What's the biggest risk environment to avoid?</strong><br>High-traffic areas with unknown dogs, like public dog parks, until your vet confirms adequate protection.</p>
