<p>Missed booster appointments happen — the real question is what it means for your pet's protection and what to do next, not whether to panic.</p>

<h2>It Usually Doesn't Mean Starting Over</h2>
<p>In many cases, your vet can resume the schedule from where you left off, though this depends on how much time has passed and which vaccine.</p>

<h2>Why Timing Still Matters</h2>
<p>A significant delay may reduce protection or require a modified catch-up approach — contact your vet promptly rather than waiting further.</p>

<h2>What to Do</h2>
<p>Call your vet as soon as you realize a booster was missed, rather than waiting for the next scheduled visit, to get specific guidance for your pet's situation.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>How late is too late for a booster?</strong><br>This varies by vaccine — your vet can advise based on the specific one missed and the delay length.</p>
<p><strong>Does a missed booster mean no protection at all?</strong><br>Not necessarily, but it can reduce confidence in protection level — check with your vet.</p>
<p><strong>Should I be worried if my adult dog missed one annual booster?</strong><br>Contact your vet for guidance rather than assuming either full protection or the need to restart.</p>
