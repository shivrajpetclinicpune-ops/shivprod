<p>A first vet visit sets the foundation for your pet's ongoing care — knowing what to expect helps it go smoothly for both of you.</p>

<h2>History and Discussion</h2>
<p>Your vet will typically ask about your pet's background, diet, any known health history, and your specific concerns.</p>

<h2>Physical Examination</h2>
<p>A full head-to-tail exam covering major body systems is standard for a first visit.</p>

<h2>Planning Ahead</h2>
<p>This visit typically establishes a vaccination and preventive care plan tailored to your pet's age, species, and lifestyle.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>What should I bring to the first visit?</strong><br>Any existing medical records, and a list of questions or concerns you have.</p>
<p><strong>Will my pet need tests at the first visit?</strong><br>This depends on age, species, and findings — not always required for a straightforward first visit.</p>
<p><strong>How long does a first visit typically take?</strong><br>Often longer than routine follow-ups, given the more extensive history-taking and planning involved.</p>
