<p>A rabbit losing weight despite normal-seeming food availability is a signal worth acting on quickly, given how fast rabbit health can decline.</p>

<h2>Common Causes</h2>
<p>Dental disease limiting eating, GI stasis, parasites, and chronic illness are common causes of weight loss in rabbits.</p>

<h2>Why It's Often Missed Early</h2>
<p>A thick coat can hide weight loss visually — regular handling and, ideally, weighing helps catch changes sooner.</p>

<h2>What to Do</h2>
<p>Any noticeable weight loss should prompt a veterinary visit for a full exam, including a dental check.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>Should I weigh my rabbit regularly?</strong><br>Yes, a kitchen scale is a simple, effective way to track trends between vet visits.</p>
<p><strong>Is dental disease a common cause?</strong><br>Yes, it's one of the most frequent underlying causes of weight loss in pet rabbits.</p>
<p><strong>Can parasites cause weight loss in rabbits?</strong><br>Yes, among other causes worth ruling out with your vet.</p>
