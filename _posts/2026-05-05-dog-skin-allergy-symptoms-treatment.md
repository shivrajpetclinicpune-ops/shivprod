<p>Itchy skin is one of the most common reasons dogs are brought in for a vet visit — and one of the most frustrating for owners because the underlying cause isn't always obvious.</p>

<h2>Common Causes</h2>
<p>Food allergies, environmental allergens (as covered in our atopy article), fleas, and contact irritants can all cause allergic skin reactions.</p>

<h2>Signs to Watch For</h2>
<p>Persistent scratching, licking, red or inflamed skin, hair loss, and recurring skin or ear infections are common signs.</p>

<h2>Treatment Approach</h2>
<p>Identifying and managing the specific trigger, alongside symptomatic relief, tends to work better than symptom management alone.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>Can allergies develop later in life, not just as a puppy?</strong><br>Yes, allergies can develop or worsen at any age.</p>
<p><strong>Is it always food-related?</strong><br>No, environmental and flea-related triggers are equally, sometimes more, common.</p>
<p><strong>Can allergies be fully cured?</strong><br>Often managed rather than cured, though good management can significantly improve comfort.</p>
