<p>Preparing appropriately for your dog's imaging appointment can make the process smoother and the results clearer.</p>

<h2>Fasting for Ultrasound</h2>
<p>Fasting is sometimes recommended before an abdominal ultrasound to improve image quality — follow your vet's specific instructions.</p>

<h2>No Fasting Usually Needed for X-Ray</h2>
<p>X-rays typically don't require fasting unless sedation is planned as part of the visit.</p>

<h2>Keeping Your Dog Calm</h2>
<p>A calm, cooperative dog generally makes for a smoother, faster imaging session with clearer results.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>Should I walk my dog before the appointment?</strong><br>A brief walk beforehand can help with calmness, unless fasting-related restrictions apply.</p>
<p><strong>Will my dog need sedation?</strong><br>This depends on temperament and the specific imaging needed — your vet will advise beforehand if likely.</p>
<p><strong>Can I stay with my dog during imaging?</strong><br>This varies by clinic and safety considerations — ask directly about their specific policy.</p>
