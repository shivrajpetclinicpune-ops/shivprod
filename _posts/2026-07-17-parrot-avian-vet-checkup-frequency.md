<p>Because parrots are experts at masking illness, scheduled checkups matter more for this species than for most companion animals.</p>

<h2>Recommended Frequency</h2>
<p>An annual exam is generally recommended for healthy adult parrots, covering a physical exam and weight tracking.</p>

<h2>Why Annual Isn't Always Enough</h2>
<p>Given how well birds hide symptoms, some owners and vets opt for more frequent wellness visits, particularly for larger, longer-lived species.</p>

<h2>Between Visits</h2>
<p>Any change in droppings, appetite, activity, or feather condition warrants an unscheduled visit regardless of the last checkup date.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>Is annual enough for a healthy young parrot?</strong><br>Generally yes, though your avian vet can advise based on species and individual history.</p>
<p><strong>Do larger parrot species need different frequency?</strong><br>Discuss with your vet — longer-lived species may benefit from closer monitoring over their lifespan.</p>
<p><strong>What does a wellness exam include?</strong><br>Typically a physical exam, weight check, and a review of diet and husbandry.</p>
