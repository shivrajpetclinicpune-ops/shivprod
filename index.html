The care journey

From the first worry to a full recovery.

  1. 01

    Something feels wrong

    Not eating, limping, a lump, a fluffed-up bird, a rabbit with no droppings. Call, WhatsApp or walk in during opening hours.

  2. 02

    Consultation

    A full physical examination by a veterinarian who handles your species every week, from a Labrador to a red-eared slider. Consultation is ₹300, or ₹400 for exotic pets.

  3. 03

    Diagnosis

    Digital X-ray with instant images, sonography, and an in-house pathology lab where CBC and biochemistry are ready in 15–20 minutes.

  4. 04

    Treatment

    Results are explained before you leave. Then medication from the on-site pharmacy, a procedure, or surgery under gaseous anaesthesia.

  5. 05

    Recovery

    Suture checks, re-tests and review visits are planned before your pet goes home, so recovery has a clear next step.

Quick answers

Before you visit.

What animals do you treat?

Dogs, cats, birds (including parrots, cockatiels, conures, budgies, lovebirds and finches), rabbits, guinea pigs, hamsters and gerbils, turtles and tortoises, and reptiles such as iguanas.

What are your timings?

Mon, Tue, Thu–Sun · 10:30 AM–1:30 PM & 3:00–8:30 PM · Wednesday closed.

How much is a consultation?

Regular consultation is ₹300 and exotic pet consultation is ₹400, payable at the clinic.

How fast are lab results?

CBC and biochemistry results from our in-house pathology lab are usually ready in 15–20 minutes, so treatment can start the same visit.

Do you handle emergencies?

Emergencies are seen during opening hours only. Please call +91 77569 65169 before you travel so the team can prepare. We do not offer after-hours or Wednesday emergency service.

How do I book?

Use the appointment form to send a WhatsApp request, message us directly, or call +91 77569 65169. Your appointment is confirmed when the clinic replies. Walk-ins are welcome during opening hours.
