Dog Health FAQs — Pune Pet Parents Ask

From sudden loss of appetite to limping and vaccination schedules, here are the dog health questions we hear most often from pet parents across Pimple Nilakh, Wakad, Aundh and PCMC.

My dog has stopped eating. Should I be worried?
Yes. If your dog refuses food for more than 24 hours, or sooner if accompanied by vomiting, diarrhoea, lethargy, or fever, a veterinary examination is recommended.
My dog is vomiting repeatedly. When should I visit the vet?
Repeated vomiting, especially with blood, weakness, or refusal to eat, requires prompt veterinary attention to prevent dehydration and identify the underlying cause.
Why does my dog have diarrhoea?
Diarrhoea can result from dietary indiscretion, parasites, infections, food allergies, or more serious diseases. Persistent diarrhoea should be evaluated by a veterinarian.
My dog is limping. Does it always need treatment?
Not always, but limping may indicate a sprain, fracture, ligament injury, arthritis, or paw injury. If it persists for more than 24 hours or is severe, veterinary examination is advised.
My dog is scratching all the time. What could be the reason?
Common causes include fleas, ticks, mites, allergies, bacterial infections, fungal infections, and hormonal disorders.
Why does my dog shake its head frequently?
Head shaking often indicates ear infections, ear mites, allergies, or foreign material inside the ear.
My dog has a lump. Should it be removed?
Not every lump requires surgery, but every new lump should be examined. Early diagnosis helps determine whether it is benign or cancerous.
When should I get my dog spayed or neutered?
The ideal age depends on breed, size, and overall health. Your veterinarian will recommend the most appropriate timing.
What is pyometra in female dogs?
Pyometra is a serious uterine infection in unspayed females and is a surgical emergency requiring immediate treatment.
My dog is having difficulty urinating. Is it an emergency?
Yes. Difficulty passing urine or complete inability to urinate is an emergency and requires immediate veterinary care.
Does my dog need dental cleaning?
Dogs with tartar, bad breath, bleeding gums, or loose teeth often benefit from professional dental cleaning under anaesthesia.
Why is my dog drinking excessive water?
Increased water intake may be associated with kidney disease, diabetes, hormonal disorders, liver disease, or certain medications.
What vaccinations does my dog need?
Vaccination schedules vary with age and lifestyle but commonly include protection against rabies, parvovirus, distemper, hepatitis, and leptospirosis.
How often should I deworm my dog?
Most adult dogs should be dewormed every three months, while puppies require more frequent deworming.
My dog has blood in its urine. What does it mean?
Blood in the urine may indicate urinary tract infection, bladder stones, trauma, or other urinary diseases and requires veterinary evaluation.
Can senior dogs undergo surgery safely?
Yes. With appropriate pre-operative testing, anaesthetic planning, and monitoring, many senior dogs safely undergo surgery.
What should I do if my dog has been hit by a vehicle?
Keep your dog as still as possible and seek emergency veterinary care immediately, even if injuries are not immediately visible.
Why is my dog coughing?
Coughing may be caused by kennel cough, heart disease, tracheal disorders, pneumonia, or other respiratory conditions.
Do you perform emergency surgeries for dogs?
Yes. Emergency procedures such as Caesarean section, foreign body removal, pyometra surgery, wound repair, and bladder surgery are performed when medically indicated at our Pimple Nilakh clinic.
Why choose Shivraj Pet Clinic for dog treatment?
We provide comprehensive medical, surgical, diagnostic, and emergency dog care in Pimple Nilakh, PCMC, using modern equipment and evidence-based veterinary medicine.

Cat Health FAQs — Pune Pet Parents Ask

Cats hide illness well, which makes knowing the warning signs even more important. Here are the cat health questions our Pimple Nilakh clinic answers most for pet parents across Pune and PCMC.

My cat has stopped eating. Is it an emergency?
Yes. Cats that stop eating, especially for more than 24 hours, are at risk of serious complications such as hepatic lipidosis and should be examined promptly.
My cat cannot urinate. What should I do?
This is a life-threatening emergency, particularly in male cats. Immediate veterinary treatment is essential.
Why is my cat vomiting frequently?
Frequent vomiting may be caused by hairballs, dietary problems, kidney disease, gastrointestinal disorders, or other medical conditions.
My cat is hiding all the time. Is something wrong?
Cats often hide when they are in pain or unwell. Sudden behavioural changes should not be ignored.
Why is my cat losing weight despite eating?
Weight loss may be associated with hyperthyroidism, diabetes, intestinal disease, parasites, or cancer.
My cat has blood in its urine. What could be causing it?
Blood in the urine may result from feline lower urinary tract disease (FLUTD), urinary tract infections, bladder stones, or stress-related cystitis.
Does my cat need dental treatment?
Cats commonly develop dental disease, which may cause pain, bad breath, drooling, and difficulty eating. Professional dental care may be required.
Why is my cat breathing rapidly?
Rapid breathing may indicate respiratory disease, heart disease, pain, fever, or fluid around the lungs and requires immediate examination.
When should I spay or neuter my cat?
The recommended timing depends on your cat's age and health. Your veterinarian can advise the most appropriate age.
My cat has diarrhoea. Should I be concerned?
Persistent diarrhoea may be caused by parasites, infections, dietary intolerance, inflammatory bowel disease, or systemic illness.
Why is my cat drinking more water than usual?
Excessive thirst may indicate kidney disease, diabetes, hyperthyroidism, or other medical conditions.
My cat has a lump. Should it be checked?
Yes. Any new lump or swelling should be evaluated early to determine whether further treatment is needed.
What vaccinations does my cat need?
Core vaccinations typically include protection against feline panleukopenia, herpesvirus, calicivirus, and rabies, depending on lifestyle and local recommendations.
How often should my cat be dewormed?
Adult cats are generally dewormed every three months, while kittens require more frequent treatment.
My cat is drooling excessively. Is that normal?
Excessive drooling may indicate dental disease, oral ulcers, poisoning, nausea, or foreign bodies in the mouth.
Can older cats safely undergo surgery?
Yes. Many senior cats successfully undergo surgery after appropriate health assessment and anaesthetic planning.
Why is my cat straining in the litter tray?
Straining may indicate constipation, urinary blockage, bladder disease, or other serious conditions that require prompt veterinary care.
My cat is sneezing continuously. Does it need treatment?
Persistent sneezing may result from viral infections, allergies, nasal polyps, foreign bodies, or dental disease.
Do you perform surgery for cats?
Yes. We perform spaying, neutering, tumour removal, bladder surgery, foreign body removal, wound repair, soft tissue surgery, and other medically indicated procedures at our Pune clinic.
Why choose Shivraj Pet Clinic for cat treatment?
We provide dedicated feline medical and surgical care with modern diagnostics, in-house laboratory facilities, digital imaging, and personalised treatment plans for every cat in Pimple Nilakh, Pune.

Pet Bird FAQs — Budgies, Cockatiels, Parrots & More

Birds mask illness instinctively, so subtle changes matter. Our Exotic Animal Centre in Pimple Nilakh treats budgies, cockatiels, lovebirds, and larger parrots across Pune.

Why is my bird sitting fluffed up all day?
A fluffed-up bird that remains inactive is often trying to conserve heat and energy. This is a common sign of illness and should be examined by an avian veterinarian as soon as possible.
Why has my bird stopped eating suddenly?
Birds have a very fast metabolism. Even a short period without eating can become life-threatening. Loss of appetite may be caused by infections, liver disease, digestive problems, or pain.
Is tail bobbing in birds normal?
No. Tail bobbing while breathing usually indicates respiratory distress or another serious illness and requires immediate veterinary attention.
Why is my bird breathing with its beak open?
Open-mouth breathing may indicate respiratory disease, overheating, or severe stress. Prompt examination is recommended.
My bird is bleeding. What should I do?
Keep your bird calm, apply gentle pressure with a clean cloth if possible, and seek veterinary care immediately. Birds can lose dangerous amounts of blood quickly.
Why are my bird's droppings green?
Green droppings may be associated with reduced food intake, liver disease, infections, or other medical conditions. A veterinary examination is needed to determine the cause.
Can birds undergo surgery safely?
Yes. With appropriate anaesthesia, monitoring, and surgical expertise, many birds can safely undergo necessary surgical procedures.
Why is my bird vomiting seeds?
Seed regurgitation or vomiting may indicate crop disorders, infections, toxins, or gastrointestinal disease and should not be ignored.
How often should my bird have a health check-up?
Healthy birds should ideally have a preventive health examination once a year, while older birds or those with medical conditions may require more frequent visits.
Do you treat all pet bird species?
Yes. We treat budgies, cockatiels, lovebirds, African greys, macaws, cockatoos, finches, canaries, and many other pet birds at our Pune Exotic Animal Centre.

Turtle & Tortoise FAQs

Shell health, UVB lighting, and husbandry questions are the most common concerns we see for pet turtles and tortoises at our Exotic Animal Centre in Pimple Nilakh, Pune.

Why is my turtle not eating?
Poor appetite can result from incorrect temperature, stress, infections, parasites, or metabolic disease. A veterinary examination can identify the underlying cause.
What is shell rot?
Shell rot is a bacterial or fungal infection of the shell that requires cleaning, medication, and correction of habitat conditions.
Why are my turtle's eyes swollen?
Swollen eyes are commonly caused by vitamin A deficiency, infections, or poor water quality.
Why is my turtle floating sideways?
Uneven floating may indicate pneumonia, excessive intestinal gas, or buoyancy disorders and should be evaluated by a veterinarian.
Can turtles develop respiratory infections?
Yes. Cold temperatures, poor husbandry, and stress commonly contribute to respiratory disease in turtles.
Does my turtle need UVB lighting?
Yes. UVB light is essential for proper calcium metabolism, healthy bones, and shell development in most pet turtles and tortoises.
Why is my turtle's shell soft?
A soft shell often indicates metabolic bone disease caused by calcium deficiency, inadequate UVB exposure, or poor nutrition.
Can turtles undergo surgery?
Yes. Surgical treatment may be required for reproductive disorders, shell injuries, abscesses, and certain internal conditions.
How often should turtles be examined?
Annual health check-ups help detect nutritional deficiencies, shell problems, and internal diseases before they become severe.
Do you treat both turtles and tortoises?
Yes. We provide medical and surgical care for aquatic turtles, tortoises, and other reptiles at our Pune clinic.

Rabbit Health FAQs

Rabbits are fast-declining patients when unwell, so early recognition matters. Here's what Pune rabbit owners ask us most at Shivraj Pet Clinic's Exotic Animal Centre.

My rabbit has stopped eating. Is it an emergency?
Yes. Rabbits that stop eating can rapidly develop gastrointestinal stasis, which is a medical emergency requiring immediate treatment.
What is gastrointestinal stasis?
It is a condition where the digestive system slows or stops moving normally. Early treatment greatly improves the chances of recovery.
Should I spay or neuter my rabbit?
Yes. Spaying and neutering help prevent reproductive diseases, reduce unwanted behaviours, and improve long-term health.
At what age should rabbits be spayed or neutered?
The ideal age depends on the rabbit's breed, sex, weight, and health. Your veterinarian can recommend the best timing.
Why are my rabbit's teeth overgrowing?
Rabbit teeth grow continuously. Poor diet, genetics, or dental disease can prevent normal wear and lead to painful overgrowth.
Can rabbits safely undergo anaesthesia?
Yes. With proper pre-operative assessment and monitoring, rabbits can safely undergo many surgical procedures.
Why is my rabbit passing very small droppings?
Small droppings often indicate reduced gut movement or inadequate food intake and should be evaluated promptly.
Can rabbits develop uterine cancer?
Yes. Unspayed female rabbits have a high risk of uterine cancer as they age, making spaying an important preventive procedure.
What should rabbits eat daily?
Unlimited grass hay, fresh water, leafy vegetables, and a measured amount of quality pellets form the basis of a healthy rabbit diet.
Do you perform rabbit surgeries?
Yes. We perform rabbit spays, neuters, tumour removal, wound repair, dental procedures, and other surgical treatments at our Pimple Nilakh clinic.

Guinea Pig Health FAQs

Guinea pigs need daily vitamin C and quick attention when appetite drops. Here's what Pune guinea pig owners ask us most often.

Why has my guinea pig stopped eating?
Guinea pigs should never go long without eating. Loss of appetite may indicate dental disease, pain, digestive problems, or other serious illnesses.
Why do guinea pigs need vitamin C?
Unlike humans, guinea pigs cannot produce vitamin C. A deficiency can cause weakness, joint pain, poor wound healing, and other health problems.
Can guinea pigs develop bladder stones?
Yes. Bladder stones are relatively common and may cause pain, blood in the urine, or difficulty urinating.
Why is my guinea pig losing weight?
Weight loss may result from dental disease, chronic illness, infections, parasites, or inadequate nutrition.
Can guinea pigs undergo surgery safely?
Yes. With proper anaesthesia and monitoring, many guinea pigs safely undergo surgery when medically necessary.
What is bumblefoot?
Bumblefoot is a painful infection of the feet caused by pressure sores, obesity, poor hygiene, or unsuitable cage flooring.
Why are my guinea pig's teeth becoming too long?
Abnormal tooth wear or dental disease can lead to overgrown teeth that interfere with eating.
How often should guinea pigs have veterinary check-ups?
Annual examinations are recommended, with more frequent visits for senior pets or those with chronic illnesses.
What foods should guinea pigs eat every day?
Good-quality hay, fresh vegetables rich in vitamin C, fresh water, and guinea pig-specific pellets are essential for good health.
Do you provide emergency treatment for guinea pigs?
Yes. Guinea pigs showing loss of appetite, breathing difficulty, severe weakness, urinary problems, or injuries should be examined immediately at our Pimple Nilakh clinic.
